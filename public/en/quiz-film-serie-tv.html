Frequently asked questions

Everything about Somto's quizzes

How does Somto's movie and TV show quiz work?

Each game is a set of questions about movies and TV shows: plots, actors, directors, details, and quotes. Answer, find out right away if you got it right, and earn XP for every correct answer.

Can I challenge my friends to the quiz?

Yes. You can send a direct challenge to a friend and compare results, even with people who aren't on Somto yet: just share the challenge invite link.

What are XP, streaks, and bonuses in the quiz?

XP is the experience points you rack up by playing. Your streak rewards playing every day, while bonuses multiply the points from your first games each day. Together, they determine your spot on the leaderboard.

Does Somto's quiz contain spoilers?

No, the quiz is designed to be quick and spoiler-free. The questions test your movie and TV show knowledge without revealing endings or plot twists.
