What it's for

Every title you watch, logged and rated

Your taste in movies deserves a place to live, grow, and tell people who you are.

On Somto, every movie and every TV show you watch becomes part of your personal library. Give it a rating, mark the title as watched, and in seconds you have an organized record of everything you've watched over the years.

Your reviews and ratings don't just sit there: they feed your profile, tell your friends about your taste, and help the community figure out whether a title is worth watching or not. No opaque algorithms, just real opinions from real people.

Frequently asked questions

Everything about ratings and reviews

How do you rate a movie or TV show on Somto?

On Somto, you give every movie or TV show a rating from 1 to 10. Mark the title as watched and your rating goes straight into your personal catalog, where you'll find everything you've watched, all in order.

Are my ratings and reviews public?

Your ratings feed your profile and can be seen by the friends who follow you, so they can get a sense of your taste. They also help the community figure out whether a title is worth the watch.

Does Somto show statistics on the titles I've watched?

Yes. From your profile you can see hours watched, favorite genres, and the number of titles you've rated — stats that show what kind of viewer you are and how your taste changes over time.

Is Somto's app for rating movies and shows free?

Yes, rating and reviewing movies and TV shows on Somto is completely free. Somto is available as a web app and on iOS, with your catalog synced across both.
