What it's for

One list for everything you want to watch

No more titles scribbled in random notes, forgotten screenshots, and recommendations lost in chat.

The watchlist is where Somto starts: every time you discover an interesting movie or TV show, you save it with a tap and find it again when the right moment comes. No more evenings spent scrolling endless catalogs without deciding.

On Somto, the watchlist isn't just a static list. Every title carries its rating, seasons, genres, and what your friends think, so when you open the list you already have everything you need to decide what to watch tonight.

Frequently asked questions

Everything about Somto's watchlist

What is Somto's watchlist?

The watchlist is your list of movies and TV shows you want to watch. On Somto you save it all in one place: every time you discover an interesting title, you add it with a tap and find it again when it's time to watch it.

How do I add a movie or show to my watchlist?

You can add a title from search or from its title page, with a single tap. It goes straight into your watchlist along with its rating, genres, seasons, and what your friends think.

Is Somto's watchlist free?

Yes, the watchlist and all of Somto's features are free. Somto is available as a web app and on iOS, and your list stays in sync across both.

Does the watchlist help me pick up shows I've started?

Yes. For TV shows, the watchlist keeps track of where you left off: when a new season comes out, you'll immediately know which episode to pick up from, with nothing to piece together from memory.
