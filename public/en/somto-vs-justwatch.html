Two different tools

Positioning: what they do and what they don't

Honestly: Somto and JustWatch solve different problems. It's worth having both.

JustWatch is a streaming search engine. Look up a title and it tells you if it's on Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, RaiPlay, or other services available in Italy, then takes you straight to the player. It's very good at that.

Somto doesn't show streaming providers. It focuses on the other half of the problem: choosing what to watch. You get a watchlist, community ratings, your friends' ratings, reviews, and an Italian community. Once you know what you want to watch, you switch to JustWatch to find out where. When you don't know what to watch, you start with Somto.

For a lot of viewers, everyday use ends up being a mix: Somto to decide, JustWatch to find the player.

Frequently asked questions

Somto vs JustWatch — FAQ

Does Somto tell me which streaming platform to watch a title on?

No, Somto doesn't show streaming providers the way JustWatch does. It's a deliberate choice: Somto focuses on the choosing part (what to watch) and the community part (ratings, friends, quizzes), not on distribution.

When does it make sense to use Somto instead of JustWatch?

Somto is for when you don't know what to watch and want to choose — starting from community ratings, friends' recommendations, and your watchlist. JustWatch comes in afterward, once you already know the title and want to find out which service has it in Italy. They're two complementary tools.

Is Somto free like JustWatch?

Yes, all of Somto's features are free: watchlist, ratings, reviews, quizzes, and stats. Somto is available as a web app and on iOS.

What does Somto have that JustWatch doesn't?

A community of Italian viewers who rate and review titles, a feed of recommendations from friends, movie and TV quizzes with XP and challenges, reviews you can read in Italian, and personal stats (hours watched, genres, how your taste evolves).
