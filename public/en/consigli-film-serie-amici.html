Why it works

The best recommendations come from friends

No algorithm knows your taste better than the people who actually know you.

When a friend tells you "you absolutely have to watch this," that recommendation beats a thousand automated suggestions. That's exactly what Somto is built around: follow people you trust and see in real time what they're watching and rating.

Every rating, every title your friends add, and every discussion they have becomes a recommendation for you. So when you don't know what to watch tonight, you already have a list of ideas from people with tastes like yours.

Frequently asked questions

Everything about recommendations from friends

How do I get movie and TV show recommendations on Somto?

On Somto, recommendations come from friends, not an algorithm. Follow people you trust, and every rating, title they add, or discussion they start becomes a suggestion on what to watch.

Can I recommend a title directly to a friend?

Yes. You can send a movie or TV show straight to a friend as a suggestion, and get their recommendations back too. It's the fastest way to pass along the right title.

How do I decide what to watch with friends?

With Match, swipe through titles with a tap and Somto quickly finds the movie or show you all agree on. No more wasted evenings deciding what to watch.

Is Somto free for discovering recommendations from friends?

Yes, following friends and getting movie and TV show recommendations is free. Somto is available as a web app and on iOS, so you stay connected to your friends from any device.
