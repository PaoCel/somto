About us

An independent project

Somto comes from a common frustration: spending evenings scrolling endless catalogues without ever really choosing. It's built by people who live for movies and TV: no opaque algorithms, no ads, no spoilers — just your taste, your friends and the drive to find something new.

It's free and stays free. Web app and iOS in sync; on Android it installs from the browser in ten seconds.
